Ethical & Practical Reflection

Step 5

Bias & Fairness

Training data skewed toward lighter skin tones (Fitzpatrick I–III) may reduce accuracy for darker complexions. Mitigation: oversample underrepresented groups and apply stratified augmentation.

Privacy

Skin images are sensitive biometric data. Images must not leave the device without explicit consent. This app processes locally — no data is retained server-side beyond the session.

Transparency

All predictions include confidence scores and top-5 alternatives. The system is labelled a decision-support tool — not a replacement for clinical diagnosis by a licensed dermatologist.

Live-Stream Risks

Continuous webcam analysis raises surveillance concerns. Mitigation: process only on-demand frames, never record or transmit video, and require explicit user opt-in each session.

Misinformation

Incorrect automated diagnoses can cause anxiety or delayed treatment. Clear disclaimers and referral messaging are displayed alongside every prediction result.

Mitigation Strategies

Diverse training data · Regular bias audits · Confidence thresholds · "Consult a doctor" messaging · GDPR-compliant data handling · Model version transparency.

⚠ Medical Disclaimer: This system is a research prototype for educational purposes only. It does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a licensed dermatologist for diagnosis and treatment.
